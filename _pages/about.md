👨🏻‍💻 I am a final-year B.Sc. student in **Mathematics and Computer Science** at **McGill University**, and an incoming **thesis master's student** in Computer Science at the **University of Toronto** (starting September 2027), supervised by [Prof. Xujie Si](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~xsi/).

📝 My research interests lie in **formal methods** and **machine learning**, in particular agentic kernel generation and optimization, machine learning for code verification, and deep learning interpretability.
